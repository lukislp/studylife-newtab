StudyLife New Tab
Connect to StudyLife

Point this at your own self-hosted StudyLife instance. New Tab is read-only - it can show your stats, but it never starts, stops, or changes anything in your account.

"studylife-newtab" needs to be registered once on your server via studylife-developers before this works - see the README.

Connected

What this extension can see

Four narrowly-scoped, read-only permissions: Whoami, TimerState.Get, Metrics.GetSummary, and CourseGoals.GetAll. No write scope is requested - a leaked key from this extension can show your stats to whoever holds it, but cannot change anything in your StudyLife account.
